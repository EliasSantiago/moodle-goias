# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-11)

- First release for Moodle 5.2.
- Graphite palette, Plus Jakarta Sans typeface, rounded cards and translucent navbar.
- Light and dark colour schemes with a navbar toggle (saved per user, follows the device by default).
- Configurable accent colour, logo, compact logo and login panel title/text.
- Split-screen login with a gradient panel.
